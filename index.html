    How it works

    A day counts toward your streak when every core habit scheduled that day is done. If no habit is core, finishing any habit counts. Rest days pause the streak without breaking it. Focus sessions started from a habit are logged against it, and you can stop early to keep the minutes you did.

    Privacy

    Everything is stored in this browser only. Nothing is sent to a server, and there are no accounts or trackers. Download a backup from Settings to move devices or keep a copy.
